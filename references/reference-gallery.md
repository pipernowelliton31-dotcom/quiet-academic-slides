## How to learn instead of tracing

When a future user asks for a new deck, first identify the information structure: juxtaposition, trajectory, matrix, proof, decision, comparison, or closing. Only then inspect the most relevant 1–3 reference images. Recreate relative scale, narrative focus, font hierarchy, color semantics, and negative space. Change page components, data, illustrations, and specific copy to fit the new subject. The skill should be recognizable to a discerning viewer, but never a carbon copy of the diffusion presentation.

## What not to learn

Do not make every deck a three-question speech or every closing slide a beach image. Do not use arbitrary 4×4 matrices, a Gaussian equation, or the exact caption pattern unless the new content justifies them. The stable DNA is **clean scientific storytelling**, not research about diffusion.
