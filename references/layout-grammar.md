# Layout grammar and page jobs

Study these as semantic composition recipes, not compulsory slide layouts. Refer to `assets/reference-pages/reference-NN.jpg` for spatial examples when available. Choose topology by message, not by page number.

## Editorial hero
Use a dominant verified image/empirical visual on one side, restrained two-line Palatino title on warm paper, one short question and quiet metadata. Avoid decorative stock images.

## Three-question arc
Align numbers, colored nodes, question headings and concise operator captions on a three-column invisible grid. Descriptions must explain rather than repeat.

## Two-lane mechanism
Separate known transformation from predicted response with a fine rule. Match colored variables to arrows and outputs; show target, prediction, and loss only when valid. Math must sit beside its actual operation.

## Sequential mechanism
Use a horizontal left-to-right state trajectory with correct directional arrows, a single anchor equation and compact operation names. Show actual intermediate states if claiming a real process. Keep the light palette.

## Relationship matrix
Use an accurately labeled and meaningful matrix when showing many-to-many interactions, with a short equation and distinct input-to-update-to-output flow. Do not route inputs directly to a final output if there are intermediate steps. Preserve full output-image aspect ratio.

## Open question
Balance one precise, serif-font research question on the left with authentic evidence on the right. Visually distinguish prior work and current experiments. Avoid unnecessary visible footnotes.

## Returning close
Visually echo the opening mood, use a large serif thanks or takeaway, a 2–3-word recap and minimal metadata. Do not force repeated imagery.

## Other composition patterns
- Benchmark: one honest chart, a concise claim, one annotated insight.
- Theorem: central true equation, aligned derivation, semantic color emphasis.
- Comparison: balanced evidence lanes sharing evaluation criteria.
- Ablation: authentic consistent output grid, labels and restrained legend.
- Longer talks: alternate reasoning-dense and quiet synthesis pages.

## Preflight
Ask: What single claim matters? Which entities and variables are real? Is the relation sequential, symmetric, causal, hierarchical or many-to-many? Which evidence is measured? Can a structured visual replace a paragraph without losing accuracy?
